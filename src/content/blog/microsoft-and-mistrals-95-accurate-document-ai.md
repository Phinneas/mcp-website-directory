## Microsoft and Mistral Launch Enterprise Document AI with 95%+ Accuracy

**The Scoop:** Microsoft Foundry now offers [Mistral Document AI 2512](https://ai.azure.com/explore/models/mistral-document-ai-2505/version/1/registry/azureml-mistral), a new enterprise-grade model that achieves **95.9% accuracy** on complex document layouts and **99%+ recognition** across multiple languages.

**Unpacked:**

*   Unlike traditional OCR that simply extracts text, this model **understands document structure** — multi-column layouts, tables with merged cells, handwritten annotations, and multilingual content — then converts everything into structured, machine-ready data.
  

  

*   The model combines **mistral-ocr-2512 for extraction** with mistral-small-2506 for understanding, enabling financial services teams to automate loan applications, healthcare organizations to process clinical records, and legal departments to analyze contracts with full structural fidelity.
  

  

*   Microsoft released ARGUS, an [open-source accelerator on GitHub](https://github.com/), that provides a complete deployment pipeline — from document ingestion to structured JSON output — so enterprises can switch between OCR providers on-the-fly through a web interface without redeployment.
  

  
**Bottom line:** This represents a major shift from document digitization to true document intelligence. Tasks that previously took days of manual review now complete in minutes, turning cost-center document operations into strategic performance levers for regulated industries.
