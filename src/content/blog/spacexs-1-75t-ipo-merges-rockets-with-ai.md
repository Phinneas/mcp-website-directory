## SpaceX Eyes Historic $1.75T IPO as Musk Merges Rockets, AI, and Social Media

**The Scoop:** [SpaceX](https://s.yimg.com/os/en/proactive_us_504/e8f5730108079f097e3faa8d9a4b9f93) targets June 12 for history's largest IPO at $1.75 trillion. Investors aren't buying rockets alone—they're getting Starlink, Grok AI, and X platform.

**The Technical Details:**

*   The offering aims to raise **$75 billion** with formal announcement expected June 4.
  
*   February's **$1.25T all-stock xAI merger** bundled Grok chatbot and X into SpaceX.
  
*   **Starlink generated $11.4B revenue** in 2025 with 63% operating margins across 10M subscribers.
  
*   Total group revenue of **$16B implies 100x price-to-sales ratio**, far above typical tech valuations.
  
*   Scottish Mortgage valued SpaceX at **$1.25T in March**, a 40% discount to IPO target.

**Why It Matters for You:** The valuation raises serious sustainability questions for decision makers evaluating AI investments. A 100x price-to-sales multiple dwarfs even premium SaaS companies trading at 10-15x revenue. Starlink's 63% margins carry the entire conglomerate, creating concentration risk for investors. The xAI integration positions SpaceX against OpenAI and Anthropic in AI infrastructure competition. This IPO will likely reset expectations for AI startup valuations across the market.

**The Bigger Picture:** Musk is betting investors will pay conglomerate premiums for space-AI-social convergence. The timing alongside his OpenAI lawsuit suggests he's building an alternative AI empire.

## First Confirmed AI-Discovered Zero-Day Wasn't From OpenAI or Anthropic

**The Scoop:** [Google's threat team](https://blog.google/threat-analysis-group/) caught attackers weaponizing a real zero-day discovered by AI. The model wasn't ChatGPT, Claude, or any major frontier AI.

**The Technical Details:**

*   The exploit was a **2FA authentication bypass** in an open-source web admin tool.
  
*   Attackers wrote a **Python script** to exploit the vulnerability at scale.
  
*   **Google detected the attack** before mass exploitation campaign launched in May 2026.
  
*   The **AI model used remains unnamed** by Google's disclosure report.
  
*   This marks the **first documented case** of AI-assisted zero-day discovery in wild attacks.

**Why It Matters for You:** Safety restrictions at major AI labs don't prevent weaponization. Smaller models without guardrails already find vulnerabilities attackers can exploit. Security teams can't rely on frontier lab safety measures alone. Defensive strategies must assume AI-assisted exploit discovery is now operational reality. The unnamed model detail matters more than the specific exploit.

**The Bigger Picture:** Attackers optimized for the path of least resistance. Frontier models built safety moats while smaller tools opened side doors. This mirrors how encryption bans fail when unrestricted alternatives exist globally.

## AI Backlash Hits Critical Mass: 70% Say Technology Moving Too Fast

**The Scoop:** Public sentiment toward AI collapsed across all demographics. Enterprise adoption now faces serious headwinds.

**The Technical Details:**

*   **70%+ of Americans** say AI advances too quickly per [Economist/YouGov polling](https://images.axios.com/SmgwDy1KQ3SllgGEi6sRqkuUW4U=/0x0:1600x900/1366x768/2026/05/15/1778879153024.png).
  
*   Negative AI views **rose from 34% to 50%** over three years.
  
*   **Only 18% of young people** (ages 14-29) feel hopeful about AI technology.
  
*   Backlash spans parties: **68% Republicans and 77% Democrats** say pace too fast.
  
*   **Record data center cancellations** in Q1 2026 due to community resistance.

**Why It Matters for You:** Sales cycles will lengthen as customers demand stronger justification for AI investments. Data center constraints directly limit compute access for training and inference workloads. Morgan Stanley flags public pushback as a binding constraint on AI infrastructure growth. Companies deploying AI must now address trust and transparency in customer conversations. The gap between executive optimism and public sentiment creates strategic blind spots.

**The Bigger Picture:** This mirrors historical tech backlash patterns like early internet privacy concerns. The industry faces a trust deficit similar to social media's reckoning.

## Kin Health Raises $9M for Patient-Side AI Notetaker That Doctors Can't Access

**The Scoop:** Kin Health built an AI notetaker FOR patients to record doctor visits. Doctors can't access it—only patients control the data.

**The Technical Details:**

*   **Three-stage processing pipeline**: transcription → clinical narrative → patient-facing summary with action items
  
*   Uses **specialized medical models** for transcription, currently training on regional accents and mask speech
  
*   **HIPAA-compliant privacy standards** but not HIPAA-certified since it's patient-facing, not provider-facing
  
*   EHR integration planned for 2026 to pull physician notes into patient's unified health graph
  
*   All data **encrypted by default** with summaries kept private unless patient chooses to share

**Why It Matters for You:** This addresses a massive gap in healthcare AI tooling. Provider-side notetakers like [Heidi Health](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/kin-health-raises-9m-to-build-an-ai-notetaker-for-patients/) generate $600M annually but leave patients out. Kin's freemium model with referral monetization mirrors GoodRx's playbook. The founding team already built and sold HeyDoctor to GoodRx. Patient-controlled data creates distribution advantage—Kin travels between specialists and health systems. Different regulatory path means faster deployment than HIPAA-certified provider tools.

**The Bigger Picture:** Healthcare AI is shifting from institution-controlled to patient-controlled data infrastructure. GoodRx proved consumers will adopt free health tools funded by referrals.
